# Can configure and safely drive a differential robot in Gazebo

The learner hand-wrote the Gazebo native DiffDrive configuration, derived the full wheel separation from two single-sided wheel offsets, reduced fixed-caster lateral friction, and preserved the intended `odom -> base_footprint` frame contract. They exercised straight and rotational motion and explicitly stopped each test. Static review independently confirmed Xacro, URDF, SDF, dependency, build, and test success, including the expected geometry, limits, frames, and caster surface. Future lessons can assume basic differential-drive geometry and Gazebo Transport fluency, while retaining the important rule that native Gazebo Sim 8 DiffDrive holds its last target velocity until zero, disable, or another command is received.
